## Example

A campaign is a long-running initiative on the project - similar to an "Epic" in
Jira-style work tracking. It is expected to require many encounters, completed over
time, before it is considered complete. For example:

> "Create the MVP", "Add Payment Processing", or a version increment such as "Release
> 2.0" - each naturally spans significant iteration and multiple encounters before it
> is done.
