Introduce an archival mechanism for campaigns and their encounters: a `.sourcebook/archive/` directory that terminal-status (`completed`/`abandoned`) campaigns and their nested encounters can be relocated into, out of the top-level `campaigns/`/`encounters/<campaign>/` directories, once they no longer need to sit alongside active work. This was deliberately deferred until now - with six completed campaigns and their encounters already accumulated, the working directories are the first place archiving actually earns its keep.

This is a structural move, not a new work-status: campaigns and encounters already reach a terminal lifecycle status (`completed`/`abandoned`) before they'd ever be archived, so archiving is orthogonal to - and layered on top of - the existing status lifecycle in `docs/workflow.md`, not a replacement for it.

Encounters under this campaign will need to work out: the concrete archive layout (mirroring `campaigns/`/`encounters/<campaign>/` under `archive/`, most likely); whether archiving a campaign requires every one of its encounters to also be terminal, given `campaign_complete`/`campaign_abandon` today only guard against *open* encounters, not draft/reviewed ones left behind; the MCP/CLI tool surface for archiving; and, critically, how `search_index.py` stays consistent - `_classify()`'s path-based mapping and `rebuild_index()`'s directory walk both currently assume the live top-level layout, and neither recognizes `archive/` today, so archived content will need either its own indexed representation or a deliberate, documented decision to drop out of the index entirely.

Resolved: there will be no unarchive operation. Archiving is one-directional by design - not an open question or a deferred follow-up, a settled decision. `add-campaign-archiving`'s Requirements previously noted the absence of an unarchive command as "a follow-up in this campaign's remaining scope"; that framing is superseded by this decision.

## Log

### Completed - 2026-08-04T06:50:44Z - John Hoff

Delivered archiving for campaigns and their encounters: a `.sourcebook/archive/` tree mirroring the live `campaigns/`/`encounters/<campaign>/` layout, entered only via `campaign_archive` (cascading to every encounter, with a guard requiring the campaign and all its encounters to already be terminal), with reads (`campaign_get`/`encounter_get`) transparently falling back to the archive location while bulk listing stays live-only. Search-index consistency was closed in two steps: first fixing a pre-existing gap where campaigns were never indexed at all, then adding an `archived` column so archived content stays indexed but is excluded from search by default (`include_archived` opts back in), with `index_status` intentionally still counting archived content in undifferentiated totals. Unarchiving was considered and explicitly rejected - archiving is one-directional by design. Four real campaigns in this repo were archived as part of verifying the work end-to-end.
