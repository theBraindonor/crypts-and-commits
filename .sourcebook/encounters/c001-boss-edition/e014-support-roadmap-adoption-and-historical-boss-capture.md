## Requirements

Add explicit, incremental adoption of an existing project's roadmap before the planning-skills encounter. The developer confirmed that both planned goals and completed historical goals must be supported for a clean CAC transition, and authorized this draft. Include already-in-progress goals as context. The contracts below are proposed defaults for independent review and developer approval; drafting does not authorize implementation.

1. Recording operation and ordinary creation. Add boss_record(name, body, summary, progress, source, message, completed_on=None, depends_on=None) with matching cac boss record NAME. progress is required and exactly planned, in_progress, or completed. source and message are required nonblank text: source may be a roadmap path/URL, issue identifier, release reference, or an explicit description of developer-supplied evidence; message explains why the record represents the existing work. Require body and the existing routing summary (maximum 500 characters) over MCP; CLI supports --body, --body-file, or editor with the same mutual-exclusion/UTF-8 semantics as boss create. CLI uses --progress, --source, --message, optional --completed-on, and repeated --depends-on. This is an explicit create-only operation, not a parser, importer that fetches sources, bulk upsert, or generic status setter. Preserve boss_create's existing proposed-only signature and behavior. Core allocates no numbers and performs no Git/network operations.
2. Representation and truthful dates. Store an optional provenance mapping on the new boss: kind=roadmap, progress (the reported progress at recording time), source, message, and completed_on (ISO YYYY-MM-DD or null). The mapping is an immutable adoption snapshot, not a second current-status machine. Normal CAC created_on/created_by and updated_on/updated_by describe the actual record operation now; never backdate them or infer a historical date. completed_on is permitted only for completed progress, may be omitted when unknown, and rejects invalid or future calendar dates (use UTC today). A completed record requires message to describe the outcome/evidence, with further success criteria, rationale, known limitations, and references in the supplied body; CAC records the developer's assertion rather than claiming to verify external implementation. Validate provenance when present while retaining legacy records without it. Do not reinterpret ordinary legacy standalone records, infer provenance, or rewrite on reads.
3. Planned and in-progress capture. Both enter as unarchived, unassigned proposed bosses, preserving their reported progress in provenance and useful context in body/summary. in_progress means work predates this record, not that CAC owns or has opened it. They remain not_ready for selection until the existing explicit make-available decision; roadmap acceptance or external activity must not silently approve CAC readiness. Normal body/summary edits, dependency changes, availability, withdrawal, assignment, and coupled lifecycle rules apply afterward. Later status changes retain the immutable provenance snapshot; consumers must not present its original progress as live current status. Record unresolved external relationships in prose until actual prerequisite records exist.
4. Historical completion. completed enters directly as an unarchived, unassigned vanquished boss with explicit provenance and no campaign. Do not create synthetic campaigns/encounters, replay transitions, infer Git integration, or attach it to an existing campaign. Historical completed goals are excluded from the new-work candidate pool and satisfy direct dependencies using the same graph-integrity rules as campaign-completed bosses. Explain that this is evidence supplied during adoption, whereas CAC campaign completion remains the normal new-work mechanism. These bosses remain frozen against ordinary body/summary/dependency edits, withdrawal, make-available, reassignment, and reactivation, consistent with completed boss protections. No historical undo or generic completed-state editing API in this encounter; mistaken completed records require the existing explicitly documented developer repair process, never assistant filesystem edits.
5. Dependencies and incremental adoption. Optional depends_on resolves existing boss refs to full canonical names at record time, including archived targets; reject mixed-alias duplicates, missing/ambiguous refs, self-links, withdrawn prerequisites, malformed lists, and cycles before writes. Validate against the existing graph without concealing damage. Capture prerequisites first; no auto-created placeholder goals or requirement to enter the entire roadmap in one session. A recorded completion is a historical outcome assertion, not a new campaign assignment: it need not replay readiness or prove all its own prerequisites were completed in CAC first. Preserve normal subsequent assignment checks and integrity propagation; missing/damaged prerequisite records still block downstream eligibility. An external link in source/body is informational, never an inferred dependency or reciprocal campaign assignment.
6. Campaign boundary and archival. Do not relax e013's draft-only membership lock or alter open/paused/completed/abandoned/archived campaign lists. References to existing CAC campaigns or outside execution belong in source/body as context; actual ownership can be established only through the existing eligible-boss/new-draft or draft-set APIs. Explicitly document that adopting an already-open legacy campaign into reciprocal boss ownership is deferred; capturing its goals does not require that ownership conversion. Extend boss_archive to allow unassigned, unarchived vanquished bosses only when their valid provenance reports completed. Retain status, provenance, body bytes, identity, and dependency satisfaction in archive. Keep ordinary withdrawn-boss archival and coupled campaign archival unchanged. No archive restoration, deletion API, campaign creation/completion side effect, or exception for arbitrary unassigned vanquished records.
7. Integrity, budgets, and recovery. Reuse canonical name/collision/path safety, lazy collection creation, audit attribution, references, Recovery, search synchronization, and fingerprint evidence. Preflight the entire record, dependency graph, metadata/identity minimum output, audit inputs, and destination before persistence. Creation and standalone archive must restore original bytes/index rows/fingerprints on failure, including later index updates or source unlink; report incomplete rollback explicitly. Preserve unrelated records and pre-existing source/index divergence. A repeated name (live or archived) is an explicit collision without writes, even when payload matches; never overwrite prior roadmap context. All get/list/candidates/search paths remain bounded, truthful, read-only, and archive-aware; provenance must be available through boss_get without silently dropping required metadata, with body truncation/hydration refs or an explicit impossible-envelope error.
8. Context and compatibility. Planned/in-progress entries appear in existing candidate discovery as proposed with source-progress context; healthy external completions must not be diagnosed as missing campaign links. Dependency explanations should distinguish externally recorded completion from completion through a campaign, retaining a boss_get hydration route within existing paging/budget limits. Existing list/get/search can recover the records, their summary/body, state, and archived visibility; no new automatic inclusion of the entire roadmap in prime. Prime continues to hydrate only actual active campaign membership. Assess whether current consumers need changes and preserve their contracts unless explicitly documented. Decide and document schema/library compatibility before implementation; optional provenance must not imply bootstrap migration or older-library understanding of the new standalone-completion/archive semantics. No blanket conversion, bootstrap, or real-project index rebuild.
9. Documentation and scope. Update docs/boss-edition.md and packaged workflow.md in the same implementation change to replace the absolute campaign-only vanquishing claim with this bounded adoption exception. Document APIs, source-progress versus current lifecycle, dates, readiness, external completion evidence, dependencies, archive/repair limitations, incremental adoption examples, and the deferred existing-campaign ownership conversion. Add a focused compatibility/adoption note to migration-guide.md; broader branching/adoption guides and planning/campaign-manager skills remain subsequent encounters. Keep workflow retrievable within DOCS_RESPONSE_BUDGET by consolidating prose, not increasing limits or discarding unrelated procedures. ## Rationale

CAC must be useful in projects that already have a roadmap, not require a blank planning slate. A future goal, work currently underway elsewhere, and an already-delivered capability all contribute context, but they should not acquire invented CAC execution history. Today boss_create always creates proposed goals and successful completion is driven by campaign_complete. Planned capture is possible, but explicit source/progress provenance and direct historical completion are missing.

The proposed record operation makes that distinction visible while keeping the existing four statuses. Planned/in-progress goals enter CAC's readiness process explicitly. Completed historical goals provide context and satisfy prerequisites without manufacturing a campaign merely to produce a vanquished status. Actual CAC recording dates remain truthful even when completion predates installation.

A single-record workflow supports gradual, developer-selected adoption. Hard dependencies resolve to real records; unrelated roadmap material can stay outside CAC. An immutable adoption snapshot explains where a goal came from while normal lifecycle state says what CAC currently considers it. This draft intentionally preserves the e013 membership lock: legacy campaign ownership conversion is a separate concern, not a silent exception hidden in roadmap capture.
