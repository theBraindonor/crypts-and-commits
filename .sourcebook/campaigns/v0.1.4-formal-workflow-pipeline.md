Establish the core workflow documents that become the source of truth for how agent skills and CLI configuration are authored and maintained, replacing ad hoc, undocumented edits to SKILL.md files and settings with a documented, repeatable process. Covers defining what a workflow document is, where it lives, and how future skill/configuration changes are expected to flow through it - informed by whatever was learned bundling skills/configuration in v0.1.3.

Also needs to tighten gaps observed in actual campaign-manager usage, where the skill's documented steps are not reliably followed:

- Region assignment is frequently skipped when creating/reviewing encounters - `encounter_assign_region` doesn't reliably get called even when an encounter clearly applies to a documented region.
- The required user confirmation before spawning the reviewer subagent (draft -> reviewed gate, step 1 of the Encounter Lifecycle review process) is occasionally bypassed - the subagent gets launched without first stopping to get explicit approval.
